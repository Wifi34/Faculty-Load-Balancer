How The Platform Works

Three streamlined steps to transition your institution from manual spreadsheets to automated academic equilibrium.

STEP 01

Configure Academic Resources

Define departmental faculty limits, subject syllabi, available lecture halls in Aryabhata Block, and specialized laboratory equipment constraints.

STEP 02

Execute algorithmic distribution logic that matches faculty subject preferences against mandatory weekly credit hours without exceeding 16-hour ceilings.

Generate Conflict-Aware Timetables
